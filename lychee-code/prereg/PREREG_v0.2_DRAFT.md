# Pre-registration v0.2 — LycheeHarvest-Sim (DRAFT, not frozen)

Status: **draft**. v0.1 (`PREREG_v0.1.md`, written 2026-09-29 before any learned-policy result) is kept unchanged. v0.2 responds to the simulated peer review (`../lit/H_mock_review.md`) and to the first learned result (an end-to-end compact behaviour-cloning policy harvested 0 of 80 closed-loop episodes). The review found that v0.1 (i) let H1 be met by chance through a six-way disjunction, (ii) confounded R1 with R0, (iii) averaged seeds away, (iv) used equivalence margins the sample sizes cannot resolve and (v) claimed "before any result is inspected" while a pilot was planned. v0.2 is frozen only when the checklist in §10 is complete; after freezing, only the deviation log (§11) is edited.

## 0. Disclosure of what has been seen
- Scripted reference policies and the scripted expert have been evaluated on `iid` (60 pairs) and, partly, on other splits (30 pairs).
- One learned policy has been evaluated before the freeze: the end-to-end compact BC policy `r1_film` (1 188 paired demonstrations, 8 epochs; benchmark v1.0): PTA 0%, no fruit detached in 80 episodes, 8 of 80 episodes with the fingertips within 5 cm of a fruit. A 60-epoch run of the same policy (`r1_film_long`) was started and is reported whatever it shows.
- The **selection-track pilot on benchmark version 1.0** (arms of §3 with one training seed, evaluated on 600 IID and 600 saliency-reversed scenes) is inspected **before the freeze**. It informs (a) whether the selector is trainable to a useful level at the sample budget, (b) which conditioning (film / late / token) the confirmatory arms use, (c) the thresholds in §5. It is reported as exploratory.
- Confirmatory data use benchmark version 1.1 (§2) and seed ranges disjoint from everything in the pilot.

## 1. Question
In command-conditioned selective harvesting, (Q1) do imitation-trained selectors whose training commands correlate with a scene cue (fruit salience) learn the cue instead of the command, and does the benchmark expose this; (Q2) do paired counterfactual demonstrations remove the shortcut, and is it the contrast, or merely scene reuse and command mix, that does so.

## 2. Benchmark version 1.1 and splits
- Code: `lychee-code/lychee/`, benchmark version `v1.1` = split names with suffix `@v11` in `lychee/splits.py::SPLITS`: ordinal commands need every fruit of the commanded maturity up to position k at least 0.2 visible (`ord_min_vis`); target visibility ≥ 0.5 everywhere except `occ_ood` (0.2–0.4) so that a guard band of 0.1 separates training and occlusion targets; seed ranges shifted by +500 000 000 relative to v1.0. Visual domain randomisation is off.
- Evaluation splits: `iid`, `occ_ood`, `density_ood`, `lang_ood` (informative only for pretrained language encoders; the from-scratch selectors have never seen its words), `attr_ood`, `saliency_rev`. Scene *i* of a split is `sample_config(name, i)`; every scene is evaluated with both commands of its pair.
- Selection-track training data (`scripts/render_select.py`): `paired` (both commands of a pair; scenes `train@v11` 0…n−1), `indep` (two independently drawn commands on the same scenes, `--mode indep`), and unpaired `rho` sets (`--rho 0 | 0.5 | 0.9`, one command per scene, target set contains the most salient fruit with probability ρ; a common seed range so that all ρ share scenes). Sample budget per arm: 8 000 labelled (scene, command) samples in the pilot (4 000 scenes × 2 for paired arms, 8 000 × 1 for unpaired arms); the confirmatory budget is fixed at the freeze.
- Sizes: `iid` and `saliency_rev` 600 scenes, other splits 300.

## 3. Arms (selection track; the conditioning is the one chosen from the pilot)
R0_ρ (unpaired; ρ = 0, 0.5, 0.9); R1 (paired); R1u (independent commands on the scenes of R1); R1-blank (R1 data, command blanked at training and test: learned language-blind floor); R1 with the other two conditionings (exploratory); privileged fruit-table selectors on the same data (exploratory reference, separates perception from command understanding); data-scaling and scene-binding arms (exploratory). Architecture, optimiser and schedule are those of `scripts/train_select.py` at the freeze commit (AdamW lr 1e-3 one-cycle, batch 64, 30 epochs, random shift ±4 px), identical across arms; no per-arm tuning. Training seeds: ≥ 3 for the arms in C1–C3.
End-to-end track: the compact BC policy is reported as a negative result; further end-to-end arms (and any VLA) are added only if the 60-epoch run harvests.

## 4. Outcomes
Co-primary: **PTA** (selected fruit ∈ target set for both commands of a pair; in the selection track the selected fruit is the fruit the scripted expert picks) and, in closed loop, PTA with the first detached fruit. Secondary: TSA, wrong-target rate (invalid selections included), collapse rate (both commands select the same fruit), language sensitivity = TSA(normal) − TSA(swap), TSA with a blank command, chance-level PTA and the excess over it, family-averaged PTA, PTA by family and by fruit count (Wilson intervals), closed-loop agreement (share of episodes in which the first detached fruit equals the selected fruit).

## 5. Confirmatory hypotheses (thresholds fixed at the freeze; the values below are the draft)
- **C1** PTA(R0_0.9) − PTA(R0_0) ≤ −0.15 on `saliency_rev`, and PTA(R0_0.9) on `saliency_rev` < PTA(R0_0.9) on `iid`; paired-bootstrap intervals exclude 0. (If not met, the paper reports that the benchmark does not expose the shortcut in this policy class.)
- **C2** PTA(R1) − PTA(R0_0.9) ≥ 0.10 on `iid` and on `saliency_rev`; interval excludes 0.
- **C3** PTA(R1) − PTA(R1u) ≥ 0.05 on `iid`; interval excludes 0.
Holm correction over C1–C3 (C1 and C2 count as two tests each: five tests in total). Everything else is exploratory, reported with Benjamini–Hochberg false-discovery control.
Falsification: if R1u is within ±0.05 of R1 (90% equivalence interval inside ±0.05, which needs the confirmatory sample size), the effect of pairing is attributed to scene reuse and command mix; if the blank-command selector is within ±0.05 of R1 the benchmark does not require the command.

## 6. Statistics
- Unit: the scene (pair). Proportions over pairs: Wilson 95% intervals; differences and per-command rates: percentile bootstrap over scenes (10 000 resamples), each scene contributing both commands; McNemar's exact test on pair-level outcomes; seeds treated as a cluster (`lychee/stats.py::cluster_bootstrap`: seeds resampled, then scenes within seeds; seed-to-seed range reported).
- Power (paired binary outcome, discordant share d = 0.25): SE ≈ √(d/n) → n = 300: 0.029; n = 600: 0.020 (a 0.05 difference is detected with power ≈ 0.7 at n = 600 and ≈ 0.4 at n = 300; 0.10 with ≈ 0.9 at 300). The 0.05-effect tests (C3) therefore use n = 600 scenes per seed and ≥ 3 seeds.
- Equivalence (falsification rules): TOST with margin ±0.05, 90% interval.

## 7. Data handling
No scene is dropped after the fact; a scene for which the sampler finds no valid configuration after 400 tries is recorded as invalid (`ok = False`) and excluded identically for all arms. Hyper-parameters are fixed before the confirmatory runs; the checkpoint is the last step of the fixed schedule. Real images (optional part): split by acquisition date and scene, never by image.

## 8. Known limitations fixed in advance
Simulation only, no physical experiments; fruit radius 3.0 cm (≈ 1.7×); planar non-colliding leaves; spring-damper stem with a geometric break rule; one fixed camera; expert demonstrations from one deterministic scripted expert (contact with non-targets in 16% / 41% of episodes at 3–10 / 11–16 fruit); five templates, one alternative surface form; the selection track reads the simulator's fruit centres to map a pointed pixel to a fruit and its PTA upper-bounds the closed-loop PTA; selectors are trained from scratch and are not VLAs.

## 9. Optional parts (included only if completed before the freeze; otherwise removed from paper and prereg)
A VLA-Adapter arm (its public LIBERO recipe unchanged, on our demonstrations; measured with the LIBERO-CF metrics — grounding rate, success rate, faithful/biased — next to PTA); simulator-abstraction sensitivity study; human validation study (200 items, 10–15 annotators; page `results/human_check_web`, scorer `scripts/score_human_check.py`).

**Not run, by scope decision (2026-09-29):** no real-robot or physical experiments, and no re-running of large public baselines (OpenVLA-OFT, OpenVLA, π0, π0.5, SmolVLA, X-VLA, SpatialVLA, Octo, GR00T, Diffusion Policy, ACT, BC-Transformer) on this benchmark. Their same-scene alternative-instruction behaviour is cited from the public LIBERO-CF evaluation of released checkpoints (Fang et al., arXiv 2602.17659); anything that is run follows the public training recipe and the LIBERO-CF measurement.
